About the Conference

Advancing healthy aging through science, medicine and innovation

This interdisciplinary program explores the convergence of artificial intelligence, aging biology, and longevity medicine. It brings together leading scientists, clinicians, technology innovators, and industry partners to advance our understanding of aging and translate scientific findings into strategies for extending healthspan.

The program emphasizes AI-driven advances in aging research, precision healthcare, nutrition, reproductive health, skin aging, rehabilitation, and age-related diseases. It also examines emerging opportunities in the longevity economy, connecting scientific innovation with clinical applications, biotechnology, and healthcare delivery.

  • ✓Keynotes from leading scientists, clinicians and technology innovators
  • ✓Main forum and parallel sessions on aging, disease and longevity medicine
  • ✓Connecting research with clinical applications and industry

Submission

Guidelines for Submission

All abstract submissions and presentations should be in English.

Before you begin, please prepare the following information

  • Presenting author's contact details: email address, full postal address, daytime and evening phone number.
  • Presentation type: poster (72 inches in width and 48 inches in length, or 72" x 48") or oral.
  • Author and co-authors' details: full first and family name(s); affiliation details: department, institution / hospital, city, state (if relevant), country.
  • Abstract:
    • Title: limited to 20 words in Sentence Structure.
    • Text: limited to 250 words (Please Note: Word count is affected when graphs/tables/images are added).
    • Topic: abstracts must be allocated to a specific topic for the Scientific Program. Please choose from the .

      Abstract Topics

      1. Longevity Interventions
      2. Stem Cells, Aging and Disease
      3. Aging, Metabolism and Disease
      4. Aging, stroke and Cardiovascular Disease
      5. Age-Related Neurodegenerative Disease
      6. Genetics, Aging and Disease
      7. System Aging and Disease
      8. Aging, Immune and Translation
      9. Aging, Protein oxidation and Disease
      10. DNA Damage and Disease
  • Tables: a maximum of 3 tables of up to 10 rows x 10 columns can be included per abstract.
  • Graphs and images: each image included in the abstract is worth 30 words. A maximum of 3 images can be included per abstract. The maximum file size of each graph/image is 500 KB and the maximum pixel size is 600(w) x 800(h) pixels. You may upload graphs in JPG, GIF or PNG format.
